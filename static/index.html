          Encryption
          
        
          Messages and files are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase and your account's own salt. The server only stores ciphertext and cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
          
        
        
          
          Change passphrase for new messages
          
          Devices must all be set to the new passphrase. Messages and files encrypted with the old one stay unreadable under the new one.
